The shopping
400g stale sourdough, torn
1 bunch cavolo nero, ribs removed
2 tins cannellini beans
1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks
1 tin plum tomatoes
A parmesan rind, if you have one
Good olive oil, for finishing
Written here  —  saved 14 August
Ribollita with winter greens
T
he Tuscan bread soup that gets better the second day — which is the whole point of making it. Cook it Sunday, eat it Monday.
The method
01
Soften the onion, carrot and celery in a generous slick of oil, slowly, until sweet rather than coloured.
02
Add the tomatoes and the parmesan rind. Simmer twenty minutes.
03
Tip in the beans with their liquid and the cavolo nero. Cook until the greens collapse.
04
Stir the torn bread through and take it off the heat. It should be thick enough to stand a spoon in.
05
Leave it overnight. Reheat, and finish each bowl with far more olive oil than feels reasonable.
